Frequently asked

The questions every parent asks first.

Is my baby's audio stored or shared? +

No. Audio is processed locally on your device, or anonymized before reaching our servers. We never store raw audio recordings. Privacy is the product.

How accurate is the cry decoding? +

Our AI averages 87% confidence across eight cry categories, including hunger, discomfort, pain (like gas), and tiredness. Accuracy improves the more you use the app — every correction teaches the model about your baby.

How old does my baby need to be? +

GuideMe works best for infants aged 0 to 12 months, when cries are the primary form of communication. Parents of older babies still use the dashboard and sleep insights — the AI decoding gets less relevant as language develops.

Does it work on both iOS and Android? +

Yes. GuideMe is built for both platforms with a shared codebase, so your partner on Android and you on iPhone stay perfectly in sync — including real-time cry alerts across both devices.

What happens after the 14-day trial? +

You can keep using all the Free features forever — manual logging, dashboard, and partner sync. Only the AI magic moment and predictive insights are behind the paywall. No awkward hard blocks, no surprise charges.

Is GuideMe a replacement for my pediatrician? +

No, and it never will be. GuideMe is a decision-support companion for normal parenting moments, not a medical device. For any medical concern, please consult your pediatrician.
